About Me

Here is how I look like!

Greetings, I am Anthony de Hoog, Back end developer, I come from Hoorn in the Netherlands, I am 21 years old, and I am studying software development at Media College Amsterdam.

I decided to pursue software development because I have been playing video games my whole life and was introduced to programming in high school. After high school, I took a gap year and worked in a restaurant washing dishes because I thought I wanted to do physical work. However, I later realized that I would prefer to work behind a computer. I also discovered that I wanted to do something more creative, so I decided that software development was what I wanted to pursue.

When I program, I see it as an opportunity to express my creativity and to apply subversions to existing genres to create something entirely new. A major inspiration for me is Zelda because I grew up with Zelda games and have always wondered how such games are made and whether I could create something similar. I always have loved how they play, the music and the story in most games. Now, when I’m coding and succeed in taking a step closer to creating a game, it’s what brings me the most joy while programming.

I can approach my work both seriously and playfully engage with other people. It mostly depends on my mood and if the other people match my personality. I still will do my best to get my work done in a serious and professional manner. I also get excited about music and good stories in films and books. I always listen to music when I am working on projects and sometimes quote from different movies and series.

I currently have worked on different projects ranging from 2d platformers, Tower defence and turn based games. Currently I am also working on a personal art project and a game project. I have a good understanding in C# and know my workarounds in Unity. I also would like to learn more Coding languages and maybe work in another branch of Software Development other than Game Development.
