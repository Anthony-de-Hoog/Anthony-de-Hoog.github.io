About Me

Here is what I look like!

Greetings, My name is Anthony de Hoog, and I am a Fullstack Developer. I am from Hoorn in the Netherlands. I am 22 years old, and I am studying software development at Media College Amsterdam.

I decided to pursue software development because I have been playing video games my whole life and was introduced to programming in high school. After high school, I took a gap year and worked in a restaurant washing dishes because I thought I wanted to do physical work. However, I later realized that I would prefer to work behind a computer. I also discovered that I wanted to do something more creative, so I decided that software development was what I wanted to pursue.

When I program, I use it to express creativity and put new twists on existing genres.

I have always loved how they play, as well as the music and stories in most games.

I can approach my work both seriously and playfully, engaging with other people. It mostly depends on my mood and whether others match my personality. I will still do my best to get my work done in a serious and professional manner.

I have worked on different projects ranging from 2D platformers to Tower Defence and turn‑based games. I have a good understanding of C# and know my way around Unity. I would also like to learn more programming languages and perhaps work in another branch of software development beyond game development.
